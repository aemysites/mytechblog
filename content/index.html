Welcome

‘OSOCO? What’s this?’, you might ask. Well, actually it’s my personal web space. Nothing more, nothing less, and if you are still wondering what OSOCO might stand for…well… when I registered the domain the idea was that it’s short for “open source community” – Stupid idea, I agree, but now I have this name and have to live with it…at least it makes people curious before they move on to other more interesting spaces. So don’t expect too much for now.
But it’s getting better – someday! Or as Ray Cokes (or was it Roy Cakes?) always said: ‘It’s crap – but we love it’.

Oh, and just for the record, my name is Carsten Ziegeler.
